The sign at the bus station said lost property could be claimed until six.

It was five fifty-eight when I asked about a red umbrella, and the man behind the counter smiled like he’d been waiting all day for someone to ask.

“Red,” he said, reaching under the desk. “There are eleven. Which one is yours?”

Why we made Storyds.

Most reading happens in the gaps now: a train ride, a queue, the ten minutes before sleep. Those minutes usually go to feeds that never end, and we close the app feeling like we read nothing at all.

We wanted those minutes to feel like finishing a book. So we started with one rule: every story has to fit in a single sitting. Up to 1,200 words, a beginning, a middle and an end, and nothing to scroll past.

Storyds is where those stories live. Anyone can write one, anyone can read one, and every story gets the same quiet page, with no ads and no follower counts deciding what’s worth reading.

  • 1,200words at most, about a six-minute read.
  • 7genres, from Romance and Fantasy to Dark.
  • 0ads. Nothing between you and the story.
  • Freeto read and to write, here and in the app.

Questions, answered.

What is Storyds?

Storyds is a free iPhone app for reading and writing short stories. Every story fits in one sitting, so you can start one on the bus and finish it before your stop.

How long is a Storyds story?

Up to 1,200 words, about a six-minute read. Many are shorter: the Quick read tab collects stories you can finish in about a minute.

Is Storyds free?

Yes. Reading and writing are free, and there are no ads.

Can I read the stories without the app?

Yes. Every story published in the app also appears on the Stories page of this site, and you don’t need an account to read.

How do I publish my own short story?

Open the Write tab in the app, write up to 1,200 words, choose a genre and add a cover photo if you like. Once you publish, it appears in the app and on this site.

Who owns the stories I publish?

You do. See the Terms of Service for what publishing lets Storyds do with your story.

Can I write dark stories?

Yes, there’s a whole Dark genre. Fiction can go to hard places; what isn’t allowed is content made only to shock or hurt. The Community Guidelines explain where the line is.

Is Storyds on Android?

Not yet. Storyds is iPhone-only for now.
