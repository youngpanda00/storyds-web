Support

Something not working, or a question about your account? We read every message.

Email us and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can.

support@storyds.app

In the app

Go to Profile › Settings › Report & feedback to send a bug report or suggestion straight from your phone.

Report a story or a writer

Open the story, tap ••• and choose Report story. To stop seeing a writer’s stories, choose Block. Reports are private: the writer isn’t told who reported them. Read the Community Guidelines for what’s allowed.

Delete your account

Go to Profile › Settings › Delete account. Your stories, loves, blocks and profile are deleted, and backups are cleared within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.
