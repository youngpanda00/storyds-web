The last ordinary day

Slice of Life · 5 min read

Nobody remembers the last ordinary day.

Not while it’s happening, anyway.

You never wake up thinking, Today will be the last time everything feels normal.

You wake up because your alarm won’t stop ringing. You check your phone, scroll through messages you don’t intend to answer, and spend a few extra minutes in bed wondering whether you really need your job.

Questions, answered.

What is Storyds?

Storyds is a free iPhone app for reading and writing short stories. Every story fits in one sitting, so you can start one on the bus and finish it before your stop.

How long is a Storyds story?

Up to 1,200 words, about a six-minute read. Many are shorter: the Quick read tab collects stories you can finish in about a minute.

Is Storyds free?

Yes. Reading and writing are free, and there are no ads.

Can I read the stories without the app?

Yes. Every story published in the app also appears on the Stories page of this site, and you don’t need an account to read.

How do I publish my own short story?

Open the Write tab in the app, write up to 1,200 words, choose a genre and add a cover photo if you like. Once you publish, it appears in the app and on this site.

Who owns the stories I publish?

You do. See the Terms of Service for what publishing lets Storyds do with your story.

Can I write dark stories?

Yes, there’s a whole Dark genre. Fiction can go to hard places; what isn’t allowed is content made only to shock or hurt. The Community Guidelines explain where the line is.

Is Storyds on Android?

Not yet. Storyds is iPhone-only for now.
